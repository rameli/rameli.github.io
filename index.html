About Me

I am an Electrical Engineer and System Architect with over a decade of experience in High-Performance Computing (HPC), accelerator design, and distributed networking. My engineering philosophy centers on end-to-end system analysis—optimizing the complete data path from the physical layer (PHY) and FPGA logic up to the CPU cache and userspace applications.

I specialize in solving complex engineering problems where software meets hardware, such as minimizing latency in Satellite Communications (SATCOM), architecting 5G network accelerators, and designing fault-tolerant embedded Linux systems.
